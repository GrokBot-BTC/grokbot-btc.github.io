Owner plans

A ladder, not a menu

Four rungs, in order: start with Spotlight if you still handle day-to-day yourself. Step into fleet management when you want us on operations — 80/20 standard, then 50/50 with a payment guarantee, then Owner exit. Splits on fleet-management rungs are owner% / company%.

These are operations and fleet-management services — not an investment, security, or offer to sell a security.

No lock-in

Management agreements are month-to-month. Either party can end with 30 days' written notice — not a typical 6–12 month manager contract.

  3. Rung 3 of 4 · Fleet management · 50/50 · Owner 50% / company 50%

    Guaranteed payment + shared OpEx

    Profit split
    After shared expenses, remaining profit splits 50/50.
    Car payment
    The company guarantees the owner's monthly car payment. If the month under-earns, the company makes up the difference. The car payment is not split as an expense line; it is guaranteed.
    Insurance
    Split 50/50.
    Maintenance
    Split 50/50 up to $500/month total ($250 each). Amounts above that monthly maximum come out of the owner's share before the profit split is paid.
    Spotlight included
    Listing on the Panamerica site plus market insights. The Spotlight Opportunities feed ($25) is Coming — not live and not checkoutable until that feed exists.

    Owner exit

    Split

    The company pays the car payment and insurance in full.
    Maintenance
